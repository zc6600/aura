## Source 2: Graph-Dependent Regret Bounds in Multi-Armed Bandits
- **Title**: Graph-Dependent Regret Bounds in Multi-Armed Bandits with Network Interference
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07555
- **Verbatim Snippet**: "We study multi-armed bandits under network interference, where each unit's reward depends on its own treatment and those of its neighbors in a given graph. This induces an exponentially large action space, making standard approaches computationally impractical. We propose a novel algorithm that uses the local graph structure to minimize regret."
- **Identified Gap / Claim**: Standard approaches face an exponentially large action space under network interference, making them computationally impractical.
